09 — Questions

The things people ask before ordering.

What does delivery cost?

Free to metro Sydney. $35 flat anywhere in regional NSW, $60 interstate. It’s quoted with your price, so there’s nothing added at the end.

My logo isn’t a proper print file. Is that a problem?

No. Send whatever you have — a JPEG off your website, a PNG with a transparent background, or a photo of your signage. Our design team redraws it at print resolution and shows you the result before anything is printed. There’s no artwork fee.

Can I really order just 1,000?

Yes, and it’s our most common order. One box is a full order here, not a sample run or a favour. It gets the same press, the same board and the same ten-day turnaround as a twenty-thousand cup job.

How fast can you go if I’m opening soon?

Ten business days from proof approval is standard, and there’s no rush fee attached to it. If your fit-out date is tighter than that, call us before you order — we can sometimes move a job up the schedule.

Are the cups recyclable or compostable?

Lining type and certification to be confirmed. This answer needs to state exactly what the cups are lined with and which standards, if any, they are certified to.

Can I mix sizes in one order?

Yes. Each size is its own box of 1,000 and its own print setup, so a 12oz and 16oz order is two boxes. Artwork is adapted to each size at no extra cost.

How do I reorder?

Email or call and say “same again”. Your artwork stays on file, so there’s no proof round the second time — it goes straight to print and you have cups ten days later.

How do I pay, and are prices GST inclusive?

Prices on this page are ex GST. Payment is on invoice before the run goes to print. Card and bank transfer both fine.
