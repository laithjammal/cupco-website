Yes. The treatment and the consultation are free, and there is no obligation to go any further afterwards.

You book in, Michelle gives you a quick call first so you know what to expect, and then someone comes to you at home at a time that suits. They look at your situation, show you first-hand what’s involved, and answer your questions. You decide afterwards, in your own time.

No. The purpose of the session is to give you the information you need. Nothing is decided for you, and you can take as long as you like.
