Questions people ask first.

Do I need any experience?

No. Most of our students have never been at the controls. Your first lesson covers everything you need.

How old do I need to be?

You can start learning before you meet the licence age. The minimum age to hold a Recreational licence is 16, and 17 for a Private licence.

Do I need a medical?

Pilots need an aviation medical certificate before flying solo. We will explain which one suits your licence and where to get it.

How long does a licence take?

The minimum hours are set by the regulator, and most students need more. Flying often and finishing ground theory early are the two biggest factors. Use the planner above for the earliest possible finish.

What should I wear to my first lesson?

Comfortable clothes, closed shoes and sunglasses. We provide the headset.
